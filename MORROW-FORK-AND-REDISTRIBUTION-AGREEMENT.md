## 4. Conditions of Distribution

The Authorized Fork or Distributor agrees to:

1. Preserve applicable copyright notices and third-party licensing notices.
2. Clearly identify the distribution as an independent fork or authorized third-party distribution.
3. Avoid representing the distribution as the official Morrow release.
4. Identify significant modifications made to Morrow.
5. Provide licensing information applicable to the distributed materials.
6. Comply with applicable third-party licenses.
7. Avoid implying that the Rights Holder endorses or supports the independent distribution unless expressly authorized.
8. Observe any additional conditions recorded in Section 3.

The Authorized Fork or Distributor must not claim ownership of Morrow's original work or the official Morrow project identity.

## 6. Branding and Project Identity

Unless separately authorized in writing, the Authorized Fork or Distributor must not:

- Use the Morrow name or logo in a manner that suggests the distribution is official.

- Use branding in a manner likely to confuse users about the origin or maintenance of the distribution.

The Rights Holder may grant separate written permission for specific uses of the Morrow name or branding.
